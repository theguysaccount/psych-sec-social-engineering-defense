# Product: Spot the Scam (by Psych Sec)

**User.** Adults who get scam texts, emails and calls, which is everyone, and especially older adults and the grown children who worry about them. Second: small-business owners and office staff, who get boss-impostor and fake-invoice scams.

**Painful job.** "Is this message real?" decided alone, under pressure, in seconds. The old rule (look for typos) stopped working once AI started writing the scams and cloning voices.

**Promise.** In two minutes, practice on ten realistic messages, see exactly which tells you missed, and leave with the four signs every scam shares.

**Wedge.** A free, no-signup test that people send to their parents and challenge friends with. Each test result is a share link; each scam type is a lesson page that answers a real search ("unpaid toll text scam", "is this USPS text real").

**Why now.** AI makes scam messages flawless and voices cloneable, the FTC's alerts in 2026 describe new twists weekly (FTC impostors deactivating phone numbers, QR codes on parking meters, task scams), and the holiday season brings the delivery and fake-store peak.

**Activation metric.** A visitor finishes a 10-message test (`test_complete`). Secondary: share clicks per completion (the loop), and lesson mini-test completions.

**First revenue event.** A business pays for the team version: the same test for its staff, with a team score and the misses by scam type. It's a link slot today (`TEAM_VERSION_URL`), switched on when separate hosting and payments exist.

**What v1 deliberately won't do.**
- Send simulated phishing to anyone. Testing people without written authorization breaks computer-misuse and wiretap laws, so v1 is self-test only.
- Store answers, accounts or emails. Nothing to breach.
- Voice cloning or any intimate or personal lures.
- Give legal or financial advice beyond published FTC and CISA guidance.
